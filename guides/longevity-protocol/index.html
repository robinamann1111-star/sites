How every claim is checked: one example from Chapter 3

What they claim

Jessie Inchauspé (“Glucose Goddess”) says to eat fibre first, protein and fat second, starches and sugars last.

What's real

In a crossover trial, 16 adults with type 2 diabetes ate the same meal in different orders. Carbs last cut the post-meal glucose rise by 53% versus carbs first. Small, short studies, mostly in people with diabetes or prediabetes.

What to do

Same plate, new order: vegetables and protein first, the bread, rice or potatoes last. It costs nothing.

Verdict in the guide: mostly true. Shukla et al., BMJ Open Diabetes Research & Care, 2017.
