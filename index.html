FAQ

Quick answers.

Do I need to record anything new?

No. Everything is cut from episodes you've already published.

Will you change what I say?

Never. Clips use your own words only. You approve the moments before I edit, and each batch gets a revision round.

Will clips take my claims out of context?

No. Every clip keeps the full sentence and its caveats, and you approve each one before it goes live.

Who owns the clips?

You do. Raw cuts and finals are yours, even if you cancel.

What do I need for the DM funnel?

A booking link or offer page. I set up the rest.

Is the DM funnel HIPAA-safe?

The DM only sends a link. It collects no health information, and booking happens on your own scheduler, so anything clinical stays inside your existing compliant system.

Who posts the clips?

You do, or I schedule them for you with access you give me. They go on your accounts, under your name.

Do you guarantee clients?

I guarantee speed and volume. From day one I track views, DMs and consult requests, so you see exactly what the clips bring in.
